## Hosting + Writing

Obviously, any podcast wouldn't be a podcast without a host (or two) to walk people through an audio presentation.
That would be me! Vian and I come up with the prompts and research the facts. 

## Web Development

As a freelance web developer, I used [Astro](https://astro.build) and [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com) to get these websites up
and running for the holiday season (also known as: getting websites done within three days of notice.)


## Branding

<img class="w-1/2 aspect-square gap-2 rounded-2xl" src="../../../assets/ham-radio-album.jpg">
Simple and elegant. That's what I usually go with.

At least for the album, I was in charge of making the album art. A stopwatch and bold text was how we wanted to make it work. Colours were chosen for the pop factor, and everything else was just whatever came to mind. And I think it looks great.

## Music
Since the last time I had to compose music for a podcast, I've bettered myself as a musician.

I tried going for some very differing sounds for this project - we needed smooth and easy pieces that would
invite our listeners in to listen again.

If you want to learn more about what went on behind the scenes, I invite you to check out [my music blog.](https://blog.lwumusic.com)
