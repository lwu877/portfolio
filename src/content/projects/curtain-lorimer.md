Curtain Lorimer isn't like any other typical project that I've done in the past.
It's an evolving character - one that ebbs and flows alongside whatever I'm working on. 

## What exactly is this?
No matter how much I love going at projects alone, I do understand that sometimes, it's beneficial
for me to work with others to help make these projects the best they can possibly be.

It's the driving force behind innovative and collaborative projects. Projects I couldn't go at alone,
like Here's a Minute. Projects where it's better to team up to help tell a new story.

It's a sandbox - one where I can test ideas that work, and toss ideas that don't. One where we can
refine, revamp, and reboot older ideas into a new direction.

## Who?
For now, this little sandbox is made up of people that I've known and worked with in the past - 
people I know can synergize with me to create unique and experimental projects.
